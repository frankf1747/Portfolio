What it taught me

Judgment calls worth keeping

01Segment before you publish.

An average across two populations that behave differently describes neither.

02The errors that don't raise errors are the expensive ones.

A join that silently drops rows is worse than one that crashes.

03Ask what a timestamp physically means.

Systems record administrative acts, not events.

04Test a metric against its own definition.

Six definitions of on-time, one narrow spread, shipped as provenance.
